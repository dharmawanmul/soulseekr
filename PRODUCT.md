# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Privacy-conscious Spotify listeners who want to turn their listening history into objects worth keeping and examine their listening taste without fabricated metrics.

## Product Purpose

Soulseekr transforms Spotify listening history into seven collectible, exportable visual artifacts and a transparent private taste dossier. Success means a listener can connect or use the clearly labelled sample archive, choose a listening window and format, preview the result, and save or explore it without losing track of where the data came from.

## Positioning

Soulseekr treats listening history as physical-media design material. The same real ranking data can become a sleeve, poster, prism, cassette, receipt, player, or pass, while the dossier separates direct Spotify fields, local calculations, and creative interpretations.

## Operating Context

The product is a single-page listening studio. Visitors can connect Spotify through PKCE or enter sample mode, switch artifact editions and time windows, play available track previews, export story and square images, copy an image, open Year in Music, and inspect the Taste Dossier.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve all seven artifact formats, sample mode, Spotify PKCE connection, time-window controls, audio previews, image exports, Year in Music, and Taste Dossier.
- Preserve keyboard behavior, dialog focus handling, reduced-motion behavior, and responsive support.
- Keep the application static and client-side with no build step or backend.
- Spotify affinity rankings are not stream counts. Do not invent total minutes, unavailable genre data, or unsupported measurements.
- Connected windows Spotify does not return remain disabled rather than receiving substituted sample data.

## Brand Commitments

- Product name: Soulseekr.
- Voice: precise, editorial, private, curious, and free of hype.
- The artifact is the focal point. Spotify green is reserved for explicit Spotify connection actions.

## Product Principles

- Make the listener's artifact, not interface chrome, the brightest and most memorable object.
- Explain what is direct data, what is calculated, and what is interpretive.
- Offer a useful sample experience without disguising sample content as a connected account.
- Preserve privacy through a static client-side architecture and least-privilege Spotify access.
- Keep every visible control functional, keyboard-operable, and understandable at phone size.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA contrast, visible keyboard focus, logical tab order, 44px touch targets, reduced-motion preferences, and layouts that remain usable at narrow widths and 200% text zoom.
