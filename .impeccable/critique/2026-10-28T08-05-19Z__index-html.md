## Design Specificity Verdict

### Independent design assessment

Soulseekr is strongly authored and product-specific. The bone-paper control leaf, dark mounted stage, catalog numbering, Bodoni/mono/sans type roles, localized grooves, and seven artifact formats create a coherent private listening folio. It does not read as a generic Spotify dashboard. The main experiential miss is that the first-run gateway hides the product's strongest proof, the mounted artifact, until after the visitor makes an authorization decision.

### Deterministic scan

The detector reported nine findings in `index.html`: eight warnings and one advisory. Two missing `src` warnings at lines 304 and 353 are likely false positives because the hidden images receive sources conditionally. Six font findings concern Space Grotesk plus five fonts outside the shell design system; several are intentional artifact-specific voices, but those exceptions should be documented and legacy imports removed. One inline `#10b981` at line 306 is a genuine color-token drift. The overused-font warning overlaps the Space Grotesk drift warning.

### Visual verification

The independent visual review reached the rendered gateway and assessed the live composition. The detector browser pass also confirmed that `#auth-gateway-modal.is-open` renders, but Windows blocked screenshot serialization and the later headless capture. No reliable overlay or screenshot was produced, so mobile geometry, runtime console output, and click-through remain source-verified rather than visually certified.

## Overall Impression

The project already has the hard part: a memorable visual world tied to its product idea. The weak point is the contract between what controls promise and what they do. Fixing the first-run and unavailable-state semantics will improve trust more than another styling pass.

## What's Working

- The asymmetric paper leaf and dark stage make the artifact the focal point and avoid the generic dashboard template.
- Sample labeling, affinity language, and privacy copy are unusually honest and avoid invented usage metrics.
- Accessibility and responsive structure are present in the architecture: skip link, semantic tab/radio groups, roving tabindex, focus trapping, Escape handling, reduced motion, 44px controls, and mobile reflow rules.

## Priority Issues

### [P1] Gateway dismissal breaks its promise

**Why it matters:** The close control says it will let users explore demo mode, but close, backdrop, and Escape only dismiss the gateway. The stage then asks the user to connect. First-timers can reasonably conclude that the sample is broken.

**Fix:** Prefer making every dismiss path enter the clearly labeled sample archive. Otherwise rename the action to “Not now” and place a persistent “Open sample archive” action in the empty stage. Use one term, preferably “sample archive,” throughout.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable onboard`

### [P1] Timeframe controls advertise unavailable behavior

**Why it matters:** Three radios look selectable, but unauthenticated clicks reopen the gateway without changing the window. “All Time” is also inaccurate for Spotify's approximate one-year long-term affinity window.

**Fix:** Provide real sample data for every window or visibly disable unavailable windows with text such as “Connect for 6 months.” Rename “All Time” to “About 1 Year.” Do not expose enabled radio semantics for an unavailable choice.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable harden`

### [P1] The first viewport hides the strongest proof

**Why it matters:** Privacy-conscious visitors are asked to authorize Spotify before seeing the distinctive artifact they are authorizing it for.

**Fix:** Show one clearly labeled sample artifact by default, then frame connection as “Make this yours.” A compact decision layer can retain the privacy facts without obscuring the product.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable onboard`

### [P2] Refresh and interruption lose sample context

**Why it matters:** The gateway-dismissed flag persists, but sample mode, selected edition, and selected window do not. A returning user can land on an empty stage with the gateway suppressed.

**Fix:** Persist and restore mode, edition, and window for the session. Add an explicit “Leave sample archive” action.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable harden`

### [P2] Recovery feedback is detached and too transient

**Why it matters:** Export and Spotify errors disappear in toasts, can include raw exception text, and provide no anchored retry action.

**Fix:** Keep toasts for confirmations. Put failures beside the stage or export control with a plain-language cause and a Retry, Connect, or alternate-download action.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable harden`

## Cognitive Load

Four of eight checks fail: chunking, one thing at a time, minimal choices, and progressive disclosure. The seven-edition selector exceeds the four-item working-memory guideline, and the full workbench exposes thirteen immediate controls across editions, windows, and export actions. Preserve all seven editions, but group them meaningfully or emphasize the selected edition and adjacent choices while exposing the complete set through a clearly labeled control.

## Persona Red Flags

- **Alex, power user:** Arrow navigation is good, but there are no direct shortcuts between edition, timeframe, playback, and export, and export is one format at a time.
- **Sam, keyboard or assistive-technology user:** An enabled radio that cannot select is a semantic mismatch; transient live-region errors may disappear before recovery; PKCE is unexplained.
- **Casey, interrupted mobile user:** Seven horizontally scrolling editions demand sustained attention, top-positioned primary actions are outside the easiest thumb zone, and refresh can lose sample context.
- **Jordan, first-timer:** PKCE, affinity rankings, Audio DNA, and 3D appear before the workflow is understood; closing the gateway does not deliver the sample experience it promises.

## Minor Observations

- Unify “Year in Music” and “Spotify Wrapped 3D Experience.”
- Reduce the visual weight of `3D`, `AUDIO DNA`, and `LOCKED` before the core artifact task is complete.
- Document artifact-specific font exceptions in `DESIGN.md`, remove unused imports, and replace inline `#10b981` with a named token.
- Verify the replacement focus treatment on `.modal-input-field` reaches 3:1 contrast.
- `overflow-x: hidden` may conceal overflow; remove it during a dedicated responsive audit to reveal the real offender.

## Questions to Consider

- What if every visitor first saw a finished sample artifact, with connection framed as “Make this yours”?
- Is closing the gateway an exit, or a third onboarding choice? The current interface claims both.
- Should all seven editions have equal prominence, or should one be recommended while every edition remains accessible?
- What would make export feel like adding an object to a collection instead of merely starting a download?
